Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Your trust matters to us. Here is how we approach the information you choose to share when contacting us or preparing an order.

1

Our Commitment to Privacy

Whisker & Wonder is a T-shirt brand inspired by creativity, compassion, and care for animals of all shapes and sizes. We aim to provide a welcoming online experience while respecting the personal information customers choose to share with us.

2

Information We Collect

When you contact us or send an order inquiry through email, you may provide your name, email address, selected T-shirt design, size, quantity, questions, requests, and any delivery or contact details needed to arrange an order.

3

How We Use Your Information

Information provided through customer communications is intended to help us respond to questions, confirm order details, communicate about arrangements, and improve our customer service.

4

Information Sharing

We only share information when necessary to handle an inquiry, arrange an order, or meet applicable legal requirements.

5

Data Protection and Retention

We aim to handle customer information responsibly and retain it only for as long as necessary to complete its intended purpose.

6

Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to ask questions about personal information you have provided or to request access, corrections, or deletion where applicable.

7

Changes to This Policy

This policy is reviewed and updated when necessary. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest version.

8

Contact Us

For privacy-related inquiries, email hello@examplewebsite142.com or visit our Contact Us page.
